Feynman technique

Explain an idea in plain language, notice gaps, then simplify again.

  2. Explain it like a beginner.

Pomodoro focus

Study in focused sessions with short breaks.

  1. Pick one task.
  2. Focus 25 minutes.
  3. Rest 5 minutes.
Active recall

Practice remembering rather than only rereading.

  1. Study a section.
  2. Hide your notes.
  3. Write what you remember.
